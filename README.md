## 📌 Project Overview

Future Akshita Company is a web-based Internship and Talent Management Platform designed to connect:

- 🎓 Students
- 🏢 Companies
- 🏫 Colleges / Institutions
- 👨‍🏫 Career Counsellors
- 🛡️ Platform Administrators

The platform manages the complete internship lifecycle:

```text
Registration
     ↓
Profile Creation
     ↓
Opportunity Discovery
     ↓
Eligibility Checking
     ↓
Application
     ↓
Shortlisting
     ↓
Interview
     ↓
Selection
     ↓
Joining
     ↓
Completion
     ↓
Digital Certificate
